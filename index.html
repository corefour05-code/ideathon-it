Overview

Built for First-Year IT Innovators

An accessible platform for 1st Year IT Department students to practice structured problem solving, teamwork, and technical presentation without needing prior hackathon experience.

01

Concept & Clarity First

Focus on defining a real problem, designing a workable user flow, and presenting the technical approach clearly. Working code is optional.

02

Collaborative Squads (2–4)

Partner with 1 to 3 peers across first-year sections. Divide responsibilities across ideation, UI wireframing, architecture, and pitching.

03

Jury & Faculty Mentorship

Present before IT Department faculty reviewers, gain constructive technical feedback, and prepare for national-level hackathons.

Guidelines & Evaluation

Rules & Judging Rubric

Review team formation limits and evaluation weightage prior to registration.

Participation Guidelines

  • 1st Year IT Exclusive: Open strictly to 1st Year students of the Department of Information Technology, PSNA College of Engineering and Technology.
  • Team Size (Min 2, Max 4): Each team must have between 2 and 4 members (1 Team Lead + 1 to 3 Team Members). Solo entries are not permitted.
  • Complete Member Records: Provide Team Name, Theme, and each member’s Full Name, Section, and Register Number.
  • Unique Membership: A student may register in only one team, verified via Register Number.
  • Original Work: All problem statements, designs, and pitch decks must be prepared originally by the participating team.

Evaluation Weightage

Problem Relevance & Clarity 25%
Innovation & Originality 30%
Technical Feasibility 25%
Presentation & Q&A 20%

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate in this Ideathon?

This edition is exclusively for 1st Year IT Department students at PSNA College of Engineering and Technology.

What is the required team size?

Every team must have a minimum of 2 members and a maximum of 4 members (1 Team Lead + 1 to 3 Team Members). Solo participation is not permitted.

Can team members be from different sections?

Yes. Each member enters their individual Section and Register Number during registration, so 1st Year IT students across sections A–D can form a team.

Do we need a finished working prototype?

No. An ideathon evaluates your problem clarity, solution architecture, feasibility, and pitch presentation. Wireframes or prototypes are welcome as an extra plus.
